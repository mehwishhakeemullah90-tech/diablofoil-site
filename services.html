Premium car wrapping

A wrap changes your car’s whole look without touching the factory paint. Every car is cleaned, decontaminated and inspected first, then high-grade film is laid panel by panel — including door jambs, bumpers and tight curves.

  • Full wraps, partial wraps, stripe kits and chrome delete
  • Design consultation to pick the colour and finish for your car
  • Helps protect against light scratches, UV and paint fading
  • Aftercare advice on cleaning and looking after the wrap

Paint Protection Film

PPF is a tough, transparent film that guards your paint against scratches, road debris, stains and UV while keeping the original look. Coverage ranges from high-impact areas to the full body, cut with precision templates.

  • Multi-stage wash and decontamination before installation
  • Self-healing film: light swirls can fade away with heat
  • Precision-cut panels with seamless, factory-looking edges
  • Resists stone chips, oxidation and chemical stains
  • Help choosing the right coverage, plus aftercare guidance

Smart painting

Smart painting repairs small areas of damage — scratches, scuffs, stone chips — without repainting the whole panel. The spot is sanded, colour-matched and micro-sprayed so the repair blends in.

  • Only the damaged area is worked on, keeping original paint
  • Digital colour matching to your existing paint
  • Shorter appointments than full-panel repairs
  • Lower cost than a traditional respray
  • Factory-level finish

Commonquestions

How long does a full car wrap take to complete?
A full car wrap typically takes 1–2 days, depending on the size of the vehicle and the complexity of the design. Every panel is aligned, bubble-free and finished with precision before we hand the car back.
Does Paint Protection Film really protect the paint?
Yes. High-quality PPF creates a durable, invisible shield against scratches, stone chips, sun damage, oxidation and everyday wear, preserving your original finish and adding long-lasting value.
Can I remove or change my wrap later?
Absolutely. Our wraps use non-damaging, professional-grade adhesives that can be removed safely without harming the factory paint, so you can switch colour, texture or design anytime.
How long does paintless dent repair take?
Most dent repairs are finished within hours, because there is no filler, sanding or repainting involved.
Is your window tint legal in the UAE?
Yes. We install UAE-compliant tint films and help you choose a shade that balances privacy, heat reduction and visibility.
