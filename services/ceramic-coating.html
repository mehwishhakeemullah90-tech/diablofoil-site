Ceramiccoating

A deep, mirror-like gloss and a hard-wearing layer that sheds water and dirt, so your car stays cleaner for longer.

Get a quote WhatsApp us

The service

What’sincluded

A nano-ceramic coating bonds to your paint to protect it from sun, oxidation and everyday wear, while adding a deep, mirror-like gloss. Each car is inspected, decontaminated and polished before the coating goes on.

  • Protection from UV, oxidation, fading and weather
  • Water and grime slide off, so the car stays cleaner for longer
  • Deep, mirror-like gloss
  • Paint correction and polishing included in the preparation

Finishes & options

  • Nano-ceramic
  • High gloss

Why it’sworth it

Ceramic coating makes everyday care easier: water and dirt slide off, the shine lasts, and the paint is protected from UV and oxidation.

Mirror-like gloss

The paint is corrected and polished first, then sealed for a deep shine.

Easier to clean

Water and grime slide off, so washing takes less effort.

UV protection

Helps prevent oxidation, fading and weather damage.

Everyday protection

A bonded nano-ceramic layer that takes everyday wear.

How it works

Every service starts with a free consultation, on WhatsApp or at the studio on Airport Road.

  1. Inspect

    We check the paint and agree how much correction it needs.

  2. Correct & polish

    Decontamination and machine polishing to lift swirls before coating.

  3. Coat

    The nano-ceramic coating is applied panel by panel and left to bond.
