The service

What’sincluded

Window film that balances looks, privacy and sun protection, cut precisely to each piece of glass and installed bubble-free. Films are UAE-compliant.

  • Blocks UV and lowers heat inside the cabin
  • Choose the shade for the privacy you want
  • Cuts glare from Dubai’s bright sun
  • Clear visibility, including at night
  • Resists peeling, cracking and fading

Finishes & options

  • UAE-compliant shades

Why it’sworth it

Dubai’s sun heats a cabin quickly and makes glare tiring. The right tint keeps the inside cooler and more private, without losing clear visibility.

Cooler cabin

Blocks UV and lowers the heat inside the car.

More privacy

Less glare

UAE-compliant

Legal films, installed bubble-free, that resist peeling and fading.

How it works
