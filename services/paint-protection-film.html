The service

What’sincluded

PPF is a tough, transparent film that guards your paint against scratches, road debris, stains and UV while keeping the original look. Coverage ranges from high-impact areas to the full body, cut with precision templates.

  • Multi-stage wash and decontamination before installation
  • Self-healing film: light swirls can fade away with heat
  • Precision-cut panels with seamless, factory-looking edges
  • Resists stone chips, oxidation and chemical stains
  • Help choosing the right coverage, plus aftercare guidance

Finishes & options

  • Gloss
  • Matte
  • Satin
  • High-impact

Why it’sworth it

Sand, stone chips and strong sun wear paint down quickly. PPF takes that wear instead, preserving your original finish and the value of the car.

Impact protection

Resists stone chips, road debris, scratches and everyday wear.

Self-healing

Light swirls and marks can fade away with heat.

UV & stain resistant

Guards against oxidation, chemical stains and sun damage.

Invisible finish

Precision-cut panels with seamless edges, in gloss, matte or satin.

How it works

Every service starts with a free consultation, on WhatsApp or at the studio on Airport Road.

  1. Choose coverage

    From high-impact areas to the full body, in the finish you prefer.

  2. Prepare

  3. Install
